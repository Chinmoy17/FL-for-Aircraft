Robust and Personalized Federated Learning for Aircraft-Engine
Prognostics: Heterogeneity and Failure-Masking Poisoning on a
Turbofan Benchmark

- Two axes of client heterogeneity studied for federated turbofan RUL prognostics.
- Personalization closes ~70% of the non-IID gap; proximal ~21%, reweighting ~10%.
- A failure-masking backdoor reaches 94.9% success with clean accuracy unchanged.
- Krum cuts backdoor success tenfold and survives coordinated 2-of-4 attacks.
- Stacking personalization and Krum restores robustness at a small accuracy cost.
